# Michigan Workforce Intelligence Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Ratified:** 2026-09-11

## I. Evidence before narrative
All material factual assertions must be traceable to source artifacts or explicit derivations. Model-generated prose is never evidence.

## II. Immutable provenance
Raw artifacts and ledger history are append-only. Corrections are new records linked by supersession; original evidence remains reconstructable.

## III. Contestability by design
Claims may be supported, challenged, contradicted, superseded, retracted, or unresolved. Competing claims can coexist. The system must preserve who/what challenged a claim, why, and with which evidence.

## IV. Semantic compatibility
Metrics may be compared or combined only when definition, unit, population, geography, time basis, and taxonomy version are compatible or an explicit transformation documents the mismatch.

## V. Primary-source preference
Official Michigan and U.S. government sources are authoritative inputs for baseline workforce statistics. Source authority does not eliminate uncertainty, methodological limitations, revisions, or contradictions.

## VI. Reproducibility
Every derived metric records formula/version and input identifiers. Reports must carry enough lineage to reproduce conclusions.

## VII. Privacy and minimization
Ingest public aggregate data by default. Restricted microdata, PII, education records, or unemployment claimant data require separate authorization, threat modeling, and data-governance review.

## VIII. Local quality gate
CI is local-first. Unit tests, static checks, source-registry validation, Spec Kit traceability, and ledger verification must pass before a commit is considered releasable.

## IX. Reasoning economy
Use deterministic transforms and cached evidence for routine work. Spend high-capability model reasoning on ambiguity, conflicts, analysis, and policy—not on mechanical ETL.

## X. Michigan first, portable core
Michigan is the first deployment. Core evidence, ledger, ontology, analytics, and MCP interfaces must remain state-portable rather than hard-coded to Michigan semantics.
