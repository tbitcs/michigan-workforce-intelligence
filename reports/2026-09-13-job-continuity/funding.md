## Combining support without promising an unlawful funding stack

**Additional program: Job Corps (U.S. DOL, individual eligibility).** The [Job Corps chapter](job-corps.md) verifies age/income and admissions screening, participant services, Michigan center listings, historical operational changes and the current availability limitation. Apply through MyJobCorps/admissions; center acceptance and a start date remain required. This is participant support, not employer grant revenue. Any complementary WIOA/local support must fund distinct approved costs and avoid duplicating center services. Employers independently verify job readiness, work authorization and role requirements; no automatic hiring subsidy is established.

**Finding:** Going PRO's employer guide contemplates braided funding through Michigan Works!. That does not authorize charging two payers for the same training expense. Its full-time rule also matters: reducing a 40-hour worker to 28 hours may conflict with the 32-hour eligibility condition at training start. Resolve timing and eligibility with both administrators before proposing Work Share plus Going PRO. The guide limits course duration and award periods; a multi-year credential is not automatically fully funded.

**Proposal:** the fiscal lead uses the following conditional allocations. These are viable structures to seek approval for, not individual legal eligibility determinations.

| Combination | Structure to seek written approval for | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| WIOA/Going PRO + employer contribution | Approved tuition or training expenses to one grant; employer pays required match/paid release time | Same invoice or wage hour claimed twice, unapproved match, preaward service |
| Apprenticeship + training support | Employer wages under standards; eligible instruction separately funded through an approved program | Treating registration as a grant, or duplicate wage reimbursement |
| Work Share + training | UIA-approved reduced hours and truthful reporting; separately approved training | Hours/availability incompatibility or training-fund full-time rule unresolved |
| Reconnect + WIOA/supports | College coordinates last-dollar tuition; eligible transportation/equipment/support separately assessed | Paying tuition already covered or assuming a noneligible course qualifies |
| TriShare + other childcare support | Hub and other payer explicitly allocate eligible costs and worker/employer shares | Duplicate payment of the same childcare cost or unapproved subsidy interaction |
| Local/philanthropic flexible fund + public programs | Sponsor covers independently approved gaps, with effects on benefits assessed | Promising UI-neutral wage supplements or assigning restricted grants to ineligible costs |

No combination is cleared merely because it appears in this table. Before enrollment, the administering agencies must confirm worker/employer eligibility, cost allocation, contribution treatment and effects on benefits in writing. The report does not have case facts sufficient to resolve every legal combination.

## Expired windows, proposals and local financing

FY26 Going PRO independent application rounds are closed; an ELC intake does not reopen those rounds. Future-year opportunities require rechecking published notices. Apprenticeship expansion applications/announcements are not pilot grant commitments. Do not place either in booked revenue.

The pilot's flexible income/benefit bridge, student coordination support, relocation assistance and pooled operating fund are **proposed expenditures** to negotiate with employers, a fiscal sponsor and local/philanthropic partners. No open municipal or county grant was verified for these uses. Local workforce agencies are delivery and eligibility partners, not a promise that each county has discretionary funding. Record an actual appropriation/award and allowable costs before including local public revenue.
