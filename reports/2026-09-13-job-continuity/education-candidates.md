Every institution in this report is a **candidate only**. No institution is represented in the pilot, has agreed to participate, or has committed seats, staff, funding, or job placements. Official program listings support consideration; they do not establish an operating partnership. Research reviewed September 13, 2026.

Lawrence Technological University, Rochester Christian University, Oakland University, and Kettering University remain candidates. ## Proposed selection and onboarding

Prioritize Macomb Community College and Oakland Community College for a first assessment of technical bridges in Macomb and Oakland counties. Assess Henry Ford College and Wayne County Community College District for Wayne County access, and Wayne State and UM-Dearborn for technical advancement and engineering student pathways. These are recommendations for evaluation, not invitations already sent or commitments obtained. Consider WMU and U-M Ann Arbor for statewide aerospace graduate retention and recruitment over a longer horizon.

For each actual receiving requisition, obtain the essential tasks and hiring requirements first. Then verify the candidate institution's current curriculum, prerequisites, schedule, accessible delivery, available seats, itemized cost, credit for prior learning, and paid work arrangement. An apprenticeship needs an actual employer sponsor; a co-op listing does not reserve a paid position. Confirm benefits, transport, childcare, admissions and worker consent separately. Do not promise aerospace/defense placement until the employer verifies role-specific requirements.

Record outreach permission and institutional acceptance separately from this public candidate catalog. Require documented scope and capacity before changing operational status; retain the historical candidate assessment and source date. The public MCP candidate lookup preserves its legacy `partner_institutions_list` name for compatibility and returns explicit candidate status. Additional candidates are a research catalog, not newly ingested university outcome records.
