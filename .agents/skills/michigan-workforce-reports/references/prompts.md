# Plain-language report templates

## Job Corps youth-to-employment pathway

Build a Michigan Job Corps pathway for eligible young people in [region]. Verify current eligibility, center programs, admissions and seat uncertainty. Connect training or near-completion students to real Michigan jobs/apprenticeships, with transition housing, transportation and first-paycheck support. Separate allowances from wages, federal services from local funding, and referrals/training from actual placements and Michigan retention. Show an incremental local budget and evaluation without claiming unverified jobs or seats.

Use these as complete requests after selecting Michigan Workforce Reports, or prefix with `$michigan-workforce-reports`. Replace bracketed details when known; otherwise the assistant states assumptions and identifies necessary missing inputs.

## Full job-saving program

Design a Michigan job-retention and workforce-growth program covering all occupations, with deeper manufacturing, engineering, aerospace and defense analysis. Prevent separations where viable, support paid internal upgrades and voluntary employer-to-employer transitions, retain students/graduates and recruit talent to Michigan. Audit stored evidence; fill gaps using current official sources. Deliver an executive brief, full report, demand/risk and transition matrices, regional priorities, verified funding matrix, 90-day pilot roadmap, low/base/high budget and outcome scenarios, and evidence appendix. Separate facts, assumptions, proposals and unavailable evidence. Do not infer shortages from ads or retention from earnings.

## Employer layoff prevention

Help a [industry] employer in [county] avoid laying off [number] workers in [occupations] around [date]. Current pay/hours are [details]. Compare reduced-hours retention, paid retraining, internal redeployment and voluntary transfers to nearby employers. Show the worker income/benefits effects, employer costs, funding eligibility, operational responsibilities and actions before separation. Label unconfirmed receiving jobs and funding.

## Worker transition and wage improvement

Build realistic Michigan transition options for a [current job] worker earning [pay], with [skills/credentials], living in [location], able to train [hours/week] and commute [limit]. Show transferable skills, remaining requirements, training cost/duration/providers, paid options, occupational wage benchmarks versus likely offer uncertainty, and routes that preserve income. Include alternatives if no higher-paid job is verified.

## Manufacturing and defense skills

Assess manufacturing, engineering, aerospace and defense pathways in [region]. Distinguish measured demand from strategic opportunities. Identify skills and credentials employers must validate, paid bridge training, supplier readiness, actual role-specific clearance/export-control requirements, and suitable non-cleared pathways. Do not equate a short certificate with an engineering degree or aviation mechanic license.

## Funding match

Find currently usable public support for [employer/worker case] in [county], with [training/support budget] and [start date]. Show applicant eligibility, allowed costs, available versus closed/proposed funding, administrator, application route, employer contributions and approvals. Suggest a cost-by-cost funding plan without double reimbursement; identify cash needed before reimbursements arrive.

## University and graduate retention

Compare academic awards and available outcomes for [institutions/programs]. Identify paid internships, co-ops, apprenticeships and first-job partnerships that could retain graduates in Michigan. Separate actual outcome evidence from proposed retention tactics. Show needed employer commitments and missing state-placement evidence; do not rank universities causally using earnings.

## Regional workforce priorities

Compare [Michigan regions] for job-retention and hiring interventions. State dataset periods, avoid mixing incompatible projections, and map industry exposure, training capacity, transport/childcare barriers and evidence gaps. Give ranked pilot candidates with reasons and conditions, not an unsupported numerical shortage score.

## Early-warning brief

Prepare a dated Michigan layoff-risk brief for [industries/regions]. Check official WARN and labor-market sources, distinguish notice dates from expected separation dates, avoid counting revisions twice, and explain WARN coverage limits. Identify the next pre-layoff actions and receiving-employer information needed. This is a one-time report unless I separately request monitoring.

## Talent attraction

Design an evidence-backed strategy to attract workers and returning Michiganders into [occupations/regions] without displacing local candidates. Require verified vacancies, realistic pay/housing/commuting comparisons, partner employment and childcare support, and a 12-month retention measurement plan. Distinguish active incentives from proposed relocation support.

## Pilot budget and evaluation

Model a [size]-worker Michigan retention/transition pilot with low/base/high assumptions. Separate direct costs, employer paid training time, funding requests and confirmed commitments. Show sustained outcomes, unemployment days avoided against an explicit counterfactual, wage changes, retention and cost per sustained and attributable outcome. Include formulas, uncertainty, risks and an evaluation design.

## Update an existing report

Update [report] as of [date]. Recheck source periods, program rules, deadlines and funding status; show what changed and which recommendations should change. Preserve the prior report, distinguish new evidence from prior assumptions, and do not silently replace missing observations.

## County jobs and economic dashboard

Produce a jobs and economic report for Macomb, Oakland, Wayne and Michigan. Audit stored evidence, show employment and unemployment history, payroll net changes and wages, inflation, demand signals and missing coverage. Distinguish resident employment, payroll jobs, gross gains/losses, advertisements and vacancies. Include charts and separately labeled projections and scenarios. Release job-continuity solutions as a companion.

## Interstate talent attraction

Compare Michigan with the U.S., Ohio, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, North Carolina, Texas, Florida and California using same-year earnings, regional price parities and housing data. Include uncertainty and historical trends. Separate occupation-specific offers from population median earnings, and price levels from inflation. Identify household costs still needed before recommending relocation; do not promise a wage or purchasing-power gain.

## Partner-led automotive continuity pilot

Design a Macomb/Oakland/Wayne pilot with APEX Accelerators, Velocity, Michigan Works!, MMTC, colleges and Job Corps as candidate partners. Verify roles and intake geography, identify unknown capacity and funding, and propose voluntary employer advance downsizing/hiring signals. Show retention, paid redeployment and confirmed external offers before separation. Test cyclical hypotheses against evidence; do not assume an 11-year automotive cycle. Separate the public analytical database from confidential employer/worker operations that require consent and access controls.

## Candidate education network

Identify candidate educational institutions for Macomb, Oakland, Wayne and statewide engineering/manufacturing/aerospace pathways. Use official program sources and explain geographic and work-based learning fit. Label every institution candidate only; none is represented or committed. Separate stored outcome coverage from seats, costs, funding, offers and participation.

## Broader hardship and outlook

Compare county/Michigan/U.S./key-state trends with U-3, U-6, participation, involuntary part-time work and housing instability. Do not add overlapping populations or assume official unemployment is false. Explain sampling/definition gaps, input periods and missing county evidence. Show supported monthly reference outlooks, holdout error and stress ranges; separate speculative drivers and sentiment from observed results.
