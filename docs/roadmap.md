# Development roadmap

## Available foundation

Versioned public evidence, source artifacts and a hash-chain audit; BLS/BEA/ACS/QCEW reference collectors; historical QWI and university outcomes; bounded public MCP discovery/trends; reference monthly outlooks; Docker PDF releases; a Rich manager; and a separate local aggregate employer exchange with tenant authorization, encryption and audited offer/consent workflows.

## Evidence priorities

Validate current Michigan occupational-projection/workbook loaders against their exact official binaries. Add reviewed gross job gains/losses, commuting and migration flows, county housing/eviction/childcare access and actual employer requisitions. Reconcile geographic hierarchies, revisions, seasonal adjustment and population controls. Keep unsupported sentiment and hardship forecasts explicitly missing.

## Education and pathways

All educational institutions are candidates only. Verify current curriculum, training costs, seat capacity and employer sponsorship before an operating partnership. Expand outcome coverage only through a reviewed ingest, keeping awards, available workers, placements and Michigan residence distinct. Add official taxonomy version crosswalks and evaluate worker-flow data without exposing individuals.

## Pilot operations and evaluation

Before real employer use, establish agreements, staffing, retention policies, isolated backup/restore tests and independent audit checkpoints. Hosted deployment, single sign-on, worker-identifiable case management and outbound integrations remain separate work. Validate warning lead times, missed events and false alarms; evaluate sustained outcomes against a counterfactual before claiming causal jobs saved. Reference paths are not a substitute for this validation.
